# Code of conduct

## Our standard

OH SHI welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Be respectful, direct, and constructive. Assume good intent while
judging ideas and changes on their evidence.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- giving actionable feedback about the work rather than the person;
- respecting privacy, source rights, and responsible disclosure;
- acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their impact;
- making room for questions and different technical perspectives; and
- keeping project spaces focused, safe, and professional.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Publishing
another person's private information or credentials is never acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents OH SHI.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method in the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing
sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed promptly and as
confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates this policy. Responses may include a private warning, temporary
limits on participation, or a permanent ban, depending on impact and pattern.
Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
